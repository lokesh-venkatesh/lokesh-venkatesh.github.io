## About Me

I am currently a fourth-year undergraduate student in the BS-MS program at the Indian Institute of Scientific Education & Research (IISER), Pune. My coursework spans across Biology, Physics, Mathematics, Data Science, the Social Sciences and Science Education. Besides my academic interests and work, I love to travel, meet new people, listen to music, and a whole range of other extracurricular activities I've not had time for during college. 

### Research Interests

My research interests broadly surround the theory and modelling of complex systems, both biological as well as social systems, with a particular focus on problems in computational neuroscience and machine learning. I am currently looking for masters' thesis opportunities around these themes for my fifth year.
